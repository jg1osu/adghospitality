ASK ALISA ↗
What is a done-for-you elopement package?

It’s a smaller wedding with coordinated planning. ADG Hospitality brings the agreed services and vendors together so you have one clear plan. We confirm the officiant, photography, florals, venue, setup, and any celebration extras in your written proposal before you book.

Can we include family and friends?

Absolutely. We plan celebrations for just the two of you and intimate weddings with up to 30 guests. Capacity depends on the venue: some Charleston public parks allow only 25 people total, including the couple. We’ll help you choose a setting that fits everyone.

How much do your Charleston elopement packages cost?

Petite starts at $3,500, Signature at $5,000, and Premier at $7,500. Your location, date, guest count, services, and design choices shape the final price. Your proposal will clearly identify inclusions, optional additions, venue charges, and applicable taxes before you commit.

What do we need to get legally married?

You’ll need a South Carolina marriage license and an authorized officiant. Charleston County offers an online application. South Carolina requires a 24-hour waiting period, and the county advises allowing two business days after a completed application for license delivery. Please review the county’s current requirements before finalizing travel.

What happens if it rains?

We discuss a weather plan when selecting your setting. Depending on the venue, that may mean an indoor alternative, a covered space, or a timing adjustment. Your proposal will explain the agreed backup plan and any related costs.

Can we plan everything from out of town?

Yes. Start with a call and share your vision online. We’ll guide the planning from there, including location considerations and the details you need for your trip. Travel, accommodation, and marriage-license arrangements are separate unless your proposal specifically includes them.

Can we customize a package?

That’s the idea. Tell us what matters most, whether it’s more time for photographs, a special setting, a beautiful dinner, or a particular floral style. We’ll explain what’s possible and confirm the scope and price in your proposal.

How far in advance should we reach out?

As soon as you have a date or season in mind. Spring and fall can be busy, and venue and vendor availability varies. Some Charleston park permits require at least 21 business days’ notice. If your date is close, inquire and we’ll check what’s possible.
