Privacy,
with care.

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit this website, inquire about an elopement, or choose to schedule a conversation.

Last updated October 11, 2026.

Who to contact

ADG Hospitality is the business receiving inquiries through this website. For questions about this notice or requests concerning your personal information, email alisa@adghospitality.com.

Information you choose to share

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, approximate guest count, package interest, and permission to contact you about your inquiry. You may also provide your partner’s name, phone number, preferred date, location preferences, a preferred time for a conversation, and a message about your plans. If you email us directly, we receive the information and attachments you include.

Please share only information relevant to planning your celebration. Do not submit payment-card details, identity documents, Social Security numbers, or marriage-license application documents through this website. If another person’s details are relevant, share them only with their permission.

How inquiry information is used

We use inquiry information to respond to your questions, discuss availability and options, prepare proposals, and communicate about the services you request. Consent on the inquiry form covers contact about that inquiry; it does not enroll you in a marketing mailing list.

If you become a client, additional information may be needed to plan and deliver your event. Any separate contract or service-specific notice will explain relevant arrangements. Inquiry details are not automatically published on the website.

Website hosting and email delivery

The website uses hosting and email services to process inquiries and deliver them to our business email inbox. Depending on the deployed setup, these include Vercel and Resend, or HostGator’s hosting and mail services. These providers process information needed to host the site, transmit your message, maintain security, and operate their services.

The inquiry form sends the information you enter to the website’s server, which validates it and passes accepted submissions to email delivery. It also uses a submission timestamp and an unobtrusive spam-detection field to help distinguish ordinary submissions from automated abuse. The hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps, and server logs.

Your email provider and our email provider also process messages under their own terms. Sending an inquiry is not a payment, reservation, or guarantee of availability.

Scheduling and other external links

If you choose to open a consultation calendar, you may be connected to an external scheduling provider. The website does not load a third-party booking calendar automatically when you arrive; you choose whether to open it. The provider’s privacy policy and terms apply to information you submit there and to any cookies or technical data it uses.

Links to government guidance, venues, and other external websites take you to services operated by other organizations. We do not control their privacy practices. Review the relevant provider’s notice before sharing personal information.

Cookies, analytics, and quick answers

This website does not install advertising trackers or analytics cookies by default. Its on-page quick-answer feature uses preset guidance in your browser; it does not send a conversation to an external artificial-intelligence service. Third-party websites you choose to visit may use their own cookies and tracking technologies.

Technical information may still be processed by the hosting service to deliver pages and protect the service. If analytics, advertising tools, or an external chat service are added in the future, this notice should be updated to describe the change and any choices offered.

Retention, access, and your choices

Inquiry correspondence may remain in our email records and in service-provider records according to the business’s needs and the providers’ practices. We do not promise a specific deletion deadline through this notice. Records may need to be kept for an ongoing client relationship, contractual obligations, dispute resolution, or applicable legal requirements.

You can ask us to correct your contact information, explain what inquiry information we hold, or delete information that is no longer needed. Email alisa@adghospitality.com with your request. We may need to verify that the request comes from the person concerned. Some records may need to be retained as described above.

If you prefer to avoid the form or an external calendar, email us directly. If you no longer want a follow-up about your inquiry, tell us by replying to our email.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website and business services change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Contact ADG Hospitality if you need clarification about how a particular service handles your information.
